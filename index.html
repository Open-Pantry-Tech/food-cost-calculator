Food cost calculator

Food cost % = plate cost ÷ menu price excluding GST × 100. Most Australian venues aim for 28–32% across the menu. This demo runs on src/foodCost.js, a dependency-free library you can use in your own code.

The food cost formula

Line cost = pack price ÷ pack size × quantity used ÷ yield Food cost % = plate cost ÷ (menu price ÷ 1.1) × 100 Menu price at target = plate cost ÷ target × 1.1

Pack size and quantity must be the same kind of unit: weight with weight, volume with volume, count with count. 1 L of cream is not 1 kg.
